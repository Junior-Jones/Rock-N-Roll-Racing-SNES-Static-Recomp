RESET/BASE MACHINE
Power-on and RESET are distinct. Reset-vector sampling is side-effect-free and untimed, so the pre-scheduler counter starts at zero and is named access_clock_sum. RESET preserves WRAM, WRAM-port position, open bus, MEMSEL, timer/output latches and ALU state while resetting CPU/reset-owned state. Base CPU-I/O, WRAM port and iterative multiply/divide are modeled. Scheduler/PPU/APU/DMA/input behavior remains named fail-closed future authority.
