# V10.2 natural gameplay validation

## Purpose

This gate exercises a real new-game route through the production Windows connector and the V10.2 static core. It is deliberately separate from discovery: the Mesen oracle remains an independent aid for S-CPU discovery and visual comparison, while the pass/fail authority here is the production static path.

## Route

The automated route follows the original game manual:

1. Wait through the startup sequence and press Start at the title screen.
2. Choose New Game.
3. Choose one player.
4. Choose Rookie difficulty.
5. Choose Snake Sanders.
6. Buy the default Marauder.
7. Choose Start Race.
8. Hold B to accelerate, steer right and left, fire the primary weapon with Y, and use boost with X.

The route captures the completed production framebuffer one second before and one second after every menu selection. It also records the starting grid, the steering/weapon/boost checks, and gameplay at 30, 60, and 120 seconds after the race transition.

## Result

Command-line evidence is in `evidence/v10_2/natural-route/static-core-final/natural-route.log`; paired screenshots are in the same directory.

- Result: PASS
- Completed frames: 10,650
- Native instructions: 131,060,242
- Native stereo audio frames: 5,678,207
- Per-video-frame audio range: 130 to 1,071 frames
- Video frames producing no audio: 0
- Connector audio overflow: 0
- Static stop: none
- Final observed state: active race gameplay, lap counter at 2, rendered track and player car visible

The large per-video-frame audio range is expected because SPC output is flushed in batches; the important transport properties are continuous total production, no zero-output video frame on this route, and no connector overflow.

## Coverage conclusion

The earlier 300-, 600-, and 1,000-frame neutral/exercise gates prove short deterministic startup stability. This natural route adds the missing end-to-end evidence: input polling, menu transitions, PPU output, vehicle gameplay, weapon/boost input, continuous APU output, and a 120-second production run all work together without falling into a static-core stop.

Those fixed-duration gates are complete historical validation, not the ongoing workflow. Current gameplay testing starts from the deterministic race-grid checkpoint documented in `V10.2-GAMEPLAY-CHECKPOINT.md` and has no fixed time limit.
